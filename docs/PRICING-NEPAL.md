# Awwtomation pricing and plan limits for Nepal

**Status:** Recommended launch pricing  
**Market:** Nepal  
**Pricing date:** September 21, 2026  
**Currency:** Nepalese rupees (NPR)

## Executive recommendation

Launch with five plans: Free, Starter, Growth, Pro, and Agency. Make **Growth at NPR 2,499/month** the highlighted plan. It is affordable enough for an active Nepali online shop, but high enough to support onboarding, infrastructure, payment fees, and customer support.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual billing | Effective monthly | Best for |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Free | NPR 0 | NPR 0 | NPR 0 | Testing one Instagram or Facebook account |
| Starter | NPR 999 | NPR 9,990/year | NPR 833 | Solo sellers and small local businesses |
| Growth | NPR 2,499 | NPR 24,990/year | NPR 2,083 | Growing shops that need CRM, broadcasts, and AI |
| Pro | NPR 5,999 | NPR 59,990/year | NPR 4,999 | Established brands and multi-person sales teams |
| Agency | NPR 14,999 | NPR 149,990/year | NPR 12,499 | Agencies and groups operating several brands |

Annual billing gives two months free, a 16.7% discount. Show the full annual amount at checkout and the effective monthly amount on the pricing card.

Prices should be displayed as **exclusive of applicable VAT or taxes** until Awwtomation's Nepal tax registration and invoicing treatment are confirmed. Do not advertise a tax-inclusive price and add tax later.

## Why these prices fit Nepal

1. **The audience is real and growing.** Nepal had 14.8 million social media user identities in October 2025, up 6.9% year over year. Instagram alone had an estimated 4.35 million users. This makes social commerce large enough to support a focused local automation product. [DataReportal, Digital 2026 Nepal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-nepal)
2. **Straight USD conversion is too expensive for the entry market.** Nepal Rastra Bank listed a USD selling rate of NPR 153.70 on September 21, 2026. A USD 15 plan is therefore about NPR 2,306 before card or tax costs. [Nepal Rastra Bank foreign exchange rates](https://www.nrb.org.np/forex/)
3. **The local service benchmark is higher-touch and carries setup fees.** One Nepal provider advertises website plus Facebook and Instagram AI assistance for NPR 17,999 setup plus NPR 2,500/month. Awwtomation can enter at NPR 999 because it is a repeatable self-serve product, while Growth at NPR 2,499 competes on much deeper automation, CRM, analytics, broadcasts, and inbox functionality. [Laxmi Softwares pricing](https://laxmisoftwares.site/ai-assistant.php)
4. **International products leave room for a Nepal-first alternative.** ManyChat's referenced plans begin at USD 14/month and USD 29/month. Respond.io currently begins at USD 79/month, with automation, broadcasts, and AI agents starting at USD 159/month. [Respond.io pricing](https://respond.io/pricing)
5. **Awwtomation's AI cost structure is favorable.** Customers connect their own OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic, or Google provider key. Awwtomation does not pay the model bill, so AI access can be plan-gated without pretending that unlimited model usage is included.

The goal is not to be the cheapest chatbot. The goal is to cost less than one missed order or a few hours of repetitive DM work each month.

## Overage and upgrade policy

Nepali small businesses need bill predictability. Do not silently enable unlimited overages.

### Default behavior

- Notify owners at 70%, 90%, and 100% of each monthly allowance.
- At 100% of outbound messages, pause new automated, broadcast, AI, and human outbound sends. Continue ingesting inbound messages, comments, contacts, and analytics.
- Existing data stays readable. Drafts remain editable. The account is never deleted because it reached a usage limit.
- Let the owner either upgrade immediately or buy a one-time message pack.
- Never charge an overage without the owner's explicit opt-in.

### Message packs

Message packs expire at the end of the current billing cycle and do not roll over.

| Pack | Price | Effective price/message | Eligible plans |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 2,500 messages | NPR 499 | NPR 0.200 | Starter and above |
| 10,000 messages | NPR 1,499 | NPR 0.150 | Growth and above |
| 50,000 messages | NPR 5,999 | NPR 0.120 | Pro and Agency |

If an account buys the same pack in two consecutive months, show the next plan as the recommended option. Packs should handle festivals, launches, and viral posts, not replace normal upgrades.

ManyChat's newer model counts contacts active during the billing month and applies overage pricing, with warnings at 70%, 90%, and 100%. It also lets customers set an overage threshold. Awwtomation should adopt the warning and customer-control principles, but retain a message counter at launch because that is what the current product meters reliably. [ManyChat Active Contacts](https://help.manychat.com/hc/en-us/articles/25800323349020-Active-Contacts)

## Trials, downgrades, and failed payments

### Trial

- Offer a **14-day Growth trial** with no card required.
- Trial limits: 1 channel, 5 active automations, 500 successful outbound messages, 2 team members, 1 AI agent, and no live broadcasts.
- Let users build and preview a broadcast during trial, but require a paid plan to send it.
- Trial ends by moving the organization to Free. Do not delete data.
- One trial per organization and verified owner account.

### Upgrade

- Apply the higher limits immediately.
- Prorate the current billing cycle where the payment provider supports it.
- Preserve the original renewal date.

### Downgrade

- Apply at the end of the paid billing period.
- Before confirmation, show exactly which channels, members, workspaces, automations, contacts, and AI agents exceed the new limits.
- Do not delete excess items. Lock creation and activation until usage is under the new limit.
- If active automations exceed the new plan, keep the owner's selected automations active and pause the rest at period end.

### Failed payment

- Keep paid access during the existing seven-day grace period.
- Notify the owner immediately, after three days, and one day before grace ends.
- After grace, apply Free sending and creation limits without deleting stored data.

## Limit-counting rules

These definitions should appear in the billing help centre and be implemented server-side.

1. **Organization scope:** all workspaces in one organization share plan limits.
2. **Channels:** every connected or reconnect-required account counts. A fully disconnected account does not count.
3. **Active automations:** only automations with active status count toward the active limit. Draft and paused automations count only toward the draft/storage limit.
4. **Messages:** reserve allowance before sending, count only after Meta accepts the send, and release the reservation after a failed or deferred send.
5. **Broadcasts:** one intended recipient consumes one broadcast-recipient unit. Only an accepted send also consumes one outbound-message unit.
6. **Contacts:** count unique stored contact records. Cross-channel contacts remain separate until Awwtomation implements verified identity merging.
7. **Members:** accepted members plus unexpired pending invitations count toward the seat limit.
8. **Workspaces:** every non-deleted workspace counts, including an empty workspace.
9. **Retention:** hide or aggregate expired analytics and logs through a scheduled retention job. Do not claim retention limits before this exists.
10. **Monthly reset:** use the subscription billing date. Free accounts reset on their signup-day anniversary. This is clearer than calendar-month resets after a mid-month purchase.

## What should remain unlimited

Within reasonable abuse and infrastructure controls, do not create artificial pricing gates for:

- inbound messages and comments;
- automation runs themselves, because successful outbound messages are already metered;
- tags and custom fields;
- inbox conversations;
- manual contact notes;
- automation tests that do not send through Meta;
- standard dashboard views;
- password, login, encryption, deletion, and privacy controls.

Avoid advertising "unlimited" without a fair-use qualification. Platform rate limits, Meta policies, safety controls, and the outbound-message allowance still apply.

## Current implementation gap

The live code currently has four tiers named Free, Starter, Pro, and Agency, priced in USD. It enforces only connected channels, total automations, successful DMs, team members, and broadcast availability. The public pricing above is therefore a product specification, not a claim that every limit is already enforced.

Before publishing this matrix, engineering should:

1. Add the Growth tier and replace USD catalogue amounts with NPR prices supported by the payment provider.
2. Decide whether Dodo Payments can settle and display NPR correctly. If not, keep checkout in USD but show an explicitly indicative NPR conversion, or integrate a Nepal-compatible payment path.
3. Change automation enforcement from total automations to separate active and draft limits.
4. Add organization-level counters for workspaces, stored contacts, broadcast recipients, AI agents, AI providers, saved segments, pipelines, and tracked links.
5. Add retention jobs and plan-aware date-range validation for logs and analytics.
6. Add feature entitlements for AI, exports, contact ownership, pipeline automation, branding, and support level.
7. Change usage periods from calendar-month UTC to subscription billing periods.
8. Add threshold notifications, an owner-controlled hard cap, and optional message packs.
9. Add downgrade selection so owners choose what stays active instead of the system pausing items unpredictably.
10. Add billing-event audit records for quota changes, pack purchases, resets, and manual overrides.

## Launch and validation plan

Treat these prices as a strong launch hypothesis, then validate them with real Nepali businesses.

### Before launch

- Interview at least 15 potential customers: five solo Instagram sellers, five established online shops, and five agencies or multi-brand operators.
- Ask what they currently spend on staff time, social media management, ManyChat, and missed-response follow-up. Do not ask only "Would you pay NPR 999?"
- Show Starter at NPR 799, NPR 999, and NPR 1,299 across separate interviews to test sensitivity without changing features.
- Pre-sell at least five paid accounts before building every proposed entitlement.

### First 90 days

Track conversion from Free to paid, trial activation, trial-to-paid conversion, messages per organization, gross margin, support time, pack purchases, and churn by plan.

Initial targets:

| Metric | Healthy launch target |
|---|---:|
| Visitor to Free signup | 5% or higher |
| Activated trial to paid | 20% or higher |
| Free to paid within 60 days | 5% or higher |
| Monthly paid churn | Below 5% |
| Gross margin before founder salary | Above 75% |
| Organizations hitting 80% of message limit | 10% to 25% |
| Support cost as share of revenue | Below 15% |

If more than 30% of paid users repeatedly hit message limits, allowances are too low or the upgrade gap is too large. If almost nobody uses 25% of their allowance, improve value communication before increasing limits.
